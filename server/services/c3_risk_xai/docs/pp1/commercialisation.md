# Commercialisation (C3 part)

**Persona.** A Sri Lankan adult with type 2 diabetes who sees a doctor every few months and
wants to understand, between visits, which of their habits and health factors matter most.

**Who uses / who pays / who benefits**
- Uses: the patient (free basic app).
- Pays: private hospitals, clinics and diabetes centres (licence for the clinician view and
  reports); possibly insurers and employer wellness programmes later.
- Benefits: patients (clear, explained guidance), clinicians (pre-visit summary), the health
  system (earlier attention to high-risk patients).
